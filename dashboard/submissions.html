What the decisions mean

READY — the form was readable ahead of time and every field has an answer; the head can submit it. HANDOFF — the form can't be read ahead (login walls, embedded apps), so Jobright autofill handles it live. SKIP — a required question had no answer in the bank (§L rule 3: never guess), so the job was skipped and the question logged as a gap.
