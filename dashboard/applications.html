What the columns mean

Score is the 0-100 fit the scorer gave the posting; Tier A/B/C is the role family (A = SOC/security analyst titles). Route is how it applies: ats and direct go automatically; native (Easy Apply) waits for you in the Manual queue. Source is where the job was found. A ×2 beside a company means two applications already sent there (three blocks a fourth); ⚑ flags a posting that contained text addressed to automation.
